Delete your Known account

Known by Udgam AI

You can delete your Known account and its data at any time, from the app or by email.

In the app (immediate)

  • Open Known and go to Settings.
  • Tap Delete account & data and confirm.

Deletion runs straight away and cannot be undone.

Without the app

Email contact@udgamai.com from any address with the subject "Delete my Known account" and the phone number you signed in with. We will confirm that the number is yours and complete the deletion within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile, including your phone number and sign-in.
  • Your Wall, all feedback about you, and your inbox.
  • Your streaks, badges and other progress.

What is kept

  • Feedback you wrote about other members stays on their Walls with your identity removed, because it is now their data.
  • Records of subscription purchases are held by Apple or Google under their own policies.
