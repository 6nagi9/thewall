Child safety standards

Known by Udgam AI · Effective 26 November 2026

Known has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any content that sexualises minors.

Adults only

Known is for people aged 18 and over. Everyone confirms their age before using the app, and accounts we find to belong to minors are removed.

What is not allowed

  • Any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers children.
  • Using Known to contact, groom or solicit minors.
  • Sharing, requesting or linking to CSAM in any form.

Feedback on Known uses fixed ratings and curated tags; free-text comments are short and pass automated moderation before anyone sees them.

Reporting

  • In the app: tap the flag on any feedback you received and choose Report. The item is hidden at once and our team reviews it. You can also Block the person who wrote it.
  • By email: contact@udgamai.com with the subject "Child safety report". You don't need a Known account to report.

What we do

We remove CSAE content and the accounts involved, preserve what the law requires, and report confirmed CSAM to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and Indian law enforcement through the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in). We cooperate with lawful requests from authorities.

Child safety contact

Udgam AI's designated contact for child safety is reachable at contact@udgamai.com.
