Known is a private, consent-first way to find out how the people around you really see you, and to grow from it.

CLAIM YOUR WALL
Sign in with your phone number and claim your own Wall. Known never creates a profile for anyone who hasn't joined and agreed.

INVITE PEOPLE WHO KNOW YOU
Share your link through your own messaging apps. Friends, colleagues and family rate you on fixed dimensions such as reliability, communication and punctuality, pick from curated tags, and can add a short comment.

STRUCTURED, NOT TOXIC
Feedback uses ratings and curated tags, not open-ended rants. Comments are moderated, and you can block, report or dispute anything that isn't fair.

YOU DECIDE WHAT IS PUBLIC
You see everything people tell you. Nothing about you is shown to others until you choose to show it, item by item. Averages appear only after enough people have responded, so no single answer can be singled out.

GROW OVER TIME
See your strengths, streaks and badges, and an openness badge that rewards you for being transparent, never a shaming score. Give thoughtful feedback to others and unlock deeper insight into your own Wall.

KNOWN PREMIUM (OPTIONAL)
- Trends: how each dimension of your reputation changes over time
- An AI-written summary of what your Wall says about you, with a personal growth plan
